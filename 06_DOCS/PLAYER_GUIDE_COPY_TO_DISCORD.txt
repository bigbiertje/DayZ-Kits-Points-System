DAYZ KITS & POINTS - PLAYER GUIDE

1. Register on the website.
2. Use your correct Steam64 ID.
3. Earn points by killing zombies or server events.
4. Open the Points Shop.
5. Buy a point kit.
6. Join the correct server.
7. Wait for the kit to be delivered.

Free kits are claimed in-game or Discord if the server enables the optional Discord bot.

Point kits are bought on the website.
